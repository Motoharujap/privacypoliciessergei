Privacy Policy for Furry Widget

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Welcome to Furry Widget! This privacy policy explains what information is collected when you use our app, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Information

When you use Furry Widget, and only if you have given consent where required, we may automatically collect:

  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the app, such as features used and screens viewed.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and pseudonymous app identifiers used for analytics.
  • Crash Reports: Technical details about app crashes, such as the device state and the error that occurred, so we can fix bugs.

Purchase Information

If you buy a premium subscription or purchase, the payment is processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We receive a record of the purchase (such as the product, purchase date, and order ID), linked to an anonymous app user identifier, so that we can unlock and restore your premium features.

Information We Do NOT Collect

Furry Widget has no user accounts and does not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, address)
  • Location data
  • Photos or media from your device
  • Contacts or phone numbers
  • Payment card details

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Analyze app usage patterns to develop new features
  • Detect and fix crashes and technical issues
  • Provide, verify, and restore premium purchases

Furry Widget does not display advertisements and does not sell your information.

Third-Party Services

Furry Widget uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

Google Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with our app. Firebase may collect device information and usage data. For more information, please visit Firebase Analytics Privacy Policy.

Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports. Crash data is retained for 90 days. For more information, please visit Privacy and Security in Firebase.

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat receives purchase records and an anonymous app user identifier. For more information, please visit RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Google Play

Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing, and app updates and reviews are handled through Google Play services. For more information, please visit Google Privacy Policy.

Data Storage

Your app preferences (such as the selected picture, layout, clock style, background, and refresh interval) are stored locally on your device only. We do not run our own servers and do not store personal data on them.

Children's Privacy

Furry Widget is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app displays furry character images and a clock and does not contain any inappropriate content.

Data Security

We value your trust and strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect any information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Decline analytics when the consent form is shown, where required in your region. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options in the app.
  • Request access to or deletion of data associated with you by contacting us at the email below. To help us locate purchase records, please include the Google Play order ID from your purchase receipt (it starts with "GPA.").
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions at any time in the Google Play Store
  • Uninstall the app at any time to stop all data collection

Because Furry Widget has no accounts, analytics and crash data are pseudonymous and may not be linkable to you. If we cannot identify data belonging to you, we will let you know.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
